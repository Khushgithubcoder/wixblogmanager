Connect Your Wix Website

Connect your Wix site by installing our app on it. We never see your Wix email or password, and you can revoke access from your Wix dashboard at any time.

You'll be sent to Wix to review the permissions (Blog, Site Media, Members) and choose the site to connect. Then you'll land back here.
